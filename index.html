About The Club

Born in the asphalt heat of Los Santos and forged in fire, the Holy Deities MC is an outlaw motorcycle club operating on pure discipline, heavy iron, and absolute loyalty. We don’t ask for respect—we claim it on two wheels.

Club Laws

  • Brotherhood First Absolute loyalty to the patch. Your brothers always come before the law or money.
  • Respect The Patch Wear the colors proudly and represent the club at all times in Los Santos.
  • Comms Required Working microphone required for rides, business operations, and Church meetings.
  • Church Order Handle all internal disputes in the Church room—never on the street.

Enlistment Requirements

To patch in with Holy Deities MC, you must meet the following criteria:

  • Active GTA Online player with a working microphone and Discord account
  • Own and ride a chopper or bagger class motorcycle
  • Willingness to start as a Prospect and earn your Full Patch
Prospect Phase: 1 Month or Less
